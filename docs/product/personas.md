# Personas

> **Draft for owner review.** Edit freely: these drive the MVP stories and priorities.

## 1. Riya: social user (`User`)
- 24, Jaipur, uses Instagram and WhatsApp daily, mostly on a mid-range Android phone with patchy 4G.
- **Wants:** follow friends and local places, discover events this weekend, share photos.
- **Frustrations:** feeds full of ads; can't find what's happening locally; privacy worries.
- **Needs from us:** fast mobile feed, simple privacy controls (public / followers / private), local discovery.

## 2. Arjun: vendor (`Vendor`, photographer)
- 31, runs a 3-person wedding photography studio in Delhi; gets most leads from Instagram DMs and referrals.
- **Wants:** steady bookings in peak season, fewer time-wasting enquiries, a portfolio that sells.
- **Frustrations:** double-bookings from juggling WhatsApp and paper calendars; clients ghosting after quotes.
- **Needs from us:** packages, an availability calendar that **prevents double booking**, structured enquiry → quote → booking, verified reviews.

## 3. Meena: local business owner (`BusinessOwner`, ice-cream parlour)
- 45, owns an ice-cream shop and a small factory supplying local stores; not very technical.
- **Wants:** more walk-ins, announce new flavours and offers, wholesale enquiries from shops.
- **Frustrations:** Google/Facebook business tools feel complicated; no idea whether posts bring customers.
- **Needs from us:** a **5-minute setup wizard**, product catalogue with offers, "near me" discovery, simple analytics (visits, enquiries).

## 4. Kabir: event host (`User` hosting events)
- 35, organises community meetups and his kids' birthday parties.
- **Wants:** one link to invite everyone, know who's coming, automatic reminders.
- **Frustrations:** RSVPs scattered across WhatsApp groups; people forget.
- **Needs from us:** create event, invite by link, RSVP with plus-ones, reminders that fire **exactly once**.

## 5. Priya & Rohan: couple planning a wedding (`User`, wedding workspace)
- 28 and 30, wedding in 8 months, families involved in every decision, budget ₹15 lakh.
- **Wants:** one place for checklist, budget, guests and vendors, shared with parents.
- **Frustrations:** spreadsheets everywhere; budget overruns discovered too late; comparing vendor quotes.
- **Needs from us:** shared workspace, budget planned vs actual, vendor shortlist with quote comparison. (Post-MVP, Phase 11.)

## 6. Sana: admin / moderator (`Admin`, `Moderator`)
- 29, part-time operations for the platform.
- **Wants:** keep the platform trustworthy: real businesses, real reviews, no spam or abuse.
- **Frustrations:** fake vendors, review bombing, unclear evidence when handling reports.
- **Needs from us:** approval queues for businesses and vendors, a report queue with context, bans, and an audit log of actions.
